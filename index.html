Which brands is Clinicon authorised to distribute?

Clinicon is an authorised Hong Kong distributor for more than 30 healthcare brands, including Solventum (previously 3M Healthcare), B. Braun, Baxter, Ethicon, Terumo, Welch Allyn, Littmann, Omron and Smith & Nephew, supplying hospitals, clinics, laboratories and government departments.

What medical supplies does Clinicon distribute?

Clinicon distributes surgical instruments, wound care, infection control, pharmaceutical refrigeration, diagnostics and medical consumables across Hong Kong. We supply hospitals, clinics, laboratories, universities and government departments. For enquiries or to place an order, message us on WhatsApp at 9320 9650.

About Clinicon

Who is Clinicon Medical Ltd.?

Clinicon Medical Limited is a Hong Kong company specializing in supplying high-quality medical products to clinics, elderly homes and hospitals. With 45 years of experience, we are dedicated to providing top-notch products and building lasting partnerships with our clients.

Our commitment to quality, integrity and professionalism — alongside transparency, ethical business practices and continuous improvement — sets us apart in the medical supply sector.

  • CommitmentEnsuring clients have access to reliable, essential medical products with tailored solutions.
  • IntegrityTransparency and ethical business practices in every interaction.
  • InnovationAdaptability that keeps us ahead in an ever-evolving industry.
