How to use

使用方法

Three routines, none of them complicated

三套用法，都不複雜

Apply once or twice a day to clean, unbroken skin. Avoid the eyes and face. That is the whole instruction — the rest is how to make it a habit.

每日一至兩次，塗於清潔、無破損的皮膚，避開眼睛及面部。說明就這麼多——其餘的，是如何養成習慣。

01

2 minutes · before training

2 分鐘 · 訓練前

The pre-session check-in

賽前自我檢查

  1. Warm the tube in your hand and take an amount the size of a five-cent coin per muscle group.把軟管在手中稍為回溫，每個肌群取約五毫硬幣大小的份量。
  2. Work it in with the heel of your hand, following the length of the muscle — calves and hamstrings for running, shoulders and lats for climbing or swimming, quads and hips for lifting.用掌根順著肌肉走向推開——跑步用小腿與膕繩肌，攀岩或游泳用肩膊與背闊肌，負重訓練用股四頭肌與髖部。
  3. Note anything that feels stiffer than usual. That information is the point of the two minutes.留意哪裡比平時更僵硬。這兩分鐘的價值，就在這個訊息。
  4. Then do a real warm-up: five to ten minutes of easy movement, building to your first working set.然後做真正的熱身：五至十分鐘輕鬆活動，逐步過渡到第一組正式訓練。
02

10 minutes · after training

10 分鐘 · 訓練後

The evening wind-down

運動後放鬆

  1. Shower first. Clean, dry skin absorbs it faster and you will use less.先洗澡。乾淨、乾爽的皮膚吸收更快，用量也更省。
  2. Work upwards along the limb in long, slow strokes, then circle the areas that took the most load.沿著肢體以長而緩慢的手勢向上推，再於受力最多的部位打圈按揉。
  3. Give it a minute to absorb before you dress. It should not feel slick.穿衣前等約一分鐘讓它吸收，皮膚不應有滑膩感。
  4. Pair it with the boring things that work: water, food, and going to bed at a reasonable hour.配合真正有效但不吸引的事：補水、進食，以及在合理的時間睡覺。
03

3 minutes · at a desk

3 分鐘 · 辦公桌前

The office reset

辦公室重設

  1. A pea-sized amount along each side of the neck and across the top of the shoulders.豌豆大小的份量，塗於頸部兩側及肩膊上方。
  2. Slow circles towards the collarbone, thumbs doing the work, jaw relaxed.以拇指緩慢向鎖骨方向打圈，放鬆下顎。
  3. Ten shoulder rolls backwards, ten chin tucks, then stand up.向後轉肩十次、收下巴十次，然後站起來。
  4. Twice a day beats once a week. Mid-morning and mid-afternoon are the natural slots.每天兩次勝過每週一次。上午與下午中段是最自然的時機。

找出你真的會持續的用法

Sensible cautions

使用注意

  • External use only. Keep away from eyes, mouth and broken skin.只供外用。避免接觸眼睛、口腔及破損皮膚。
  • Not recommended for children under 6.不建議 6 歲以下兒童使用。
  • If pregnant or breastfeeding, ask your doctor before use.懷孕或哺乳期間，請先諮詢醫生。
  • Patch test on the inner forearm if you have sensitive skin.皮膚敏感者請先於前臂內側試用。
  • Stop if the skin becomes red or irritated.如皮膚出現泛紅或不適，請停止使用。
  • Severe, sudden or post-injury pain needs a doctor, not a cream.劇烈、突發或受傷後的疼痛，請求醫，而非依賴按摩膏。
