Five simple steps

Find your product code

Every item in our brochures and on our website has a product code. Quoting the code is the fastest and most accurate way to order and avoids any mix-up over sizes or variants.

Choose how to reach us

WhatsApp 9320 9650 is fastest. You can also email info@clinicon.com.hk or call +852 2730 1883, Monday to Friday, 9:00am–6:00pm.

Send us your order

Include the product code(s) and name(s), quantity, delivery address and contact person, plus any account or billing reference.

We confirm your order

We reply with price, availability and the expected delivery date, and issue a written confirmation. Your order is accepted once we confirm in writing or dispatch the goods.

Delivery & payment

Delivery within Hong Kong is free on orders over HK$500. Pay by bank transfer, cheque, FPS or cash; approved account customers have 30-day terms.
