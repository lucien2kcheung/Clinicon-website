Product details

Versatile primary dressing made 100% of carboxymethylcellulose (CMC) for moderate to heavy exudate. It absorbs and retains exudate, forming a gel that conforms to the wound bed; the stitchless design helps it fit closely. Used for: Moderate to heavily exuding wounds: leg ulcers, pressure ulcers, diabetic foot ulcers, surgical wounds, cavity wounds, superficial and partial-thickness burns, trauma wounds, cancer wounds.
