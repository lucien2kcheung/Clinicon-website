Product details產品詳情

Primary or secondary super-absorbent dressing using Exu-Safe technology. Soft non-woven on both sides, a horizontal wicking layer that spreads thin and thick exudate through the whole pad, a super-absorbent core that locks fluid in, and heat-sealed edges. Available non-adhesive or with an adhesive border. Used for: Moderate to heavily exuding acute and chronic wounds, incl. leg ulcers, pressure ulcers and diabetic foot ulcers.

Kerramax Care™ 超吸收性敷料是一種主要或次要敷料，採用先進的 Exu-Safe™ 技術來改善患者護理。
