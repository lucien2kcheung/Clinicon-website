Ordering & pricing

How do I place an order with Clinicon?

You can order by WhatsApp on 9320 9650, by email to info@clinicon.com.hk, or by phone on +852 2730 1883. Tell us the product name or code and the quantity, and we will confirm price, availability and delivery. Orders are accepted once we issue a written confirmation or dispatch the goods.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

There is no minimum order quantity. Free delivery within Hong Kong applies to orders over HK$500. For orders of HK$500 or below, delivery can be arranged case by case at the applicable courier cost, which we will confirm before processing your order.

Do you offer bulk or volume discounts?

Yes. Bulk pricing is quote-based rather than a fixed published threshold, because rates vary by product and by current supply cost. Contact us with the item and quantity you need and we will provide an up-to-date quotation.

How long is a quotation valid?

Quotations are valid for 30 days from the date of issue unless we state otherwise, and remain subject to availability.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept bank transfer, cheque, FPS and cash. Approved account customers have 30 days from the invoice date to pay. First-time customers and certain goods may require payment in full before dispatch.

Do I need to open a trade account to buy from Clinicon?

No. A trade account is not required to place an order. If you would like credit terms or regular account billing, contact us and we will advise on what is needed for your organisation.

Delivery

What are your delivery lead times?

Consumables are generally delivered within one week. Lead times for equipment vary with stock availability at the manufacturer, so please enquire for a specific item and we will confirm the expected date before you order.

Is delivery free?

Delivery within Hong Kong is free on orders over HK$500. For orders of HK$500 or below we can arrange delivery at the applicable courier cost, advised to you before the order is processed.

Do you deliver outside Hong Kong?

We deliver within Hong Kong as standard. Deliveries outside Hong Kong are possible by prior arrangement — contact us with the destination and we will advise on feasibility and cost.

What should I do when the delivery arrives?

Inspect the goods on delivery and check they are correct, complete and undamaged. Notify us in writing of any shortage, incorrect item or transit damage within 7 days of delivery. After 7 days the goods are deemed accepted.

Products, service & compliance

Which brands does Clinicon distribute?

Clinicon is an authorised Hong Kong distributor for more than 40 healthcare brands, including Solventum (previously 3M Healthcare), B. Braun, Baxter, Ethicon, Terumo, Welch Allyn, Littmann, Omron, Smith & Nephew, Medline, Covidien and Swann-Morton.

Do you install and service the equipment you sell?

Yes. Clinicon handles installation, calibration and servicing for the majority of the equipment we supply, including pharmaceutical refrigeration and diagnostic equipment. Contact us with the model concerned and we will confirm what is covered.

Do I need to be a licensed healthcare provider to purchase?

Certain goods may be supplied only to qualified healthcare professionals, licensed institutions or authorised purchasers. We may ask for proof of eligibility before accepting an order, and may decline to supply where eligibility cannot be established.

Does Clinicon participate in government and Hospital Authority tenders?

Yes, for the products and categories we carry. To invite us to tender, email info@clinicon.com.hk, message us on WhatsApp at +852 9320 9650, or call +852 2730 1883.

What happens if a product is recalled?

In the event of a manufacturer or regulatory recall, field safety notice or advisory, we will contact affected customers with instructions, which may include ceasing use and returning or quarantining the goods. We ask customers to assist us and the manufacturer in reaching affected end-users.

Returns

Can I return a product?

If goods arrive defective, damaged or incorrect, we will repair, replace or refund them at no additional cost, provided you notify us within 7 days of delivery. Contact info@clinicon.com.hk or +852 2730 1883 for return instructions first — goods returned without prior authorisation may not be accepted. Other returns must be unused, unopened and in resaleable condition.

Which products cannot be returned?

For safety, hygiene and product integrity, the following cannot be returned or refunded unless defective: sterile or single-use products once opened or the sterile seal is broken; temperature-sensitive or cold-chain products such as refrigerated items; products that have been used, installed, mixed or altered; and custom, special-order or made-to-order items.

For anything not covered above, see our Terms and Conditions or contact us directly.
