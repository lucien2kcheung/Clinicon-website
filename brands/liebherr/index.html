Liebherr

Liebherr was founded in 1949 by Hans Liebherr in Kirchdorf, Germany, and its first product was a mobile tower crane. It moved into refrigeration in the 1950s: the first Liebherr refrigerator was built in 1954 at Ochsenhausen in southern Germany, and series production began a year later.

Today Liebherr's appliance division also makes laboratory and pharmacy refrigerators, used in hospitals, clinics, pharmacies and research laboratories.
