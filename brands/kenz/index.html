Kenz

Kenz is a cardiology brand of Suzuken, a Japanese company headquartered in Nagoya. According to distributors, the brand has been on the market since the mid-1970s and is sold in more than 50 countries.

It is best known for electrocardiographs (ECG machines) and ECG accessories, and its products are made at Suzuken's plant in Shenzhen, China.
