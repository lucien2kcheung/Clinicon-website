3M

3M was founded in 1902 in Two Harbors, Minnesota, as the Minnesota Mining and Manufacturing Company. Its first venture was a mining operation that struggled, and its first successful product was sandpaper. Over the following century it grew into a global science company known for adhesives, abrasives, safety equipment, electronics, filtration and consumer products.

In healthcare, 3M became a familiar name through products such as Tegaderm dressings, Micropore tape and Littmann stethoscopes. In April 2024 3M completed the spin-off of its health care business as a separate company, Solventum, so many of the wound care, tape and infection prevention products we supply are now made by Solventum, although some packaging still carries the 3M name.
