Helping your clinic meet the Code of Practice for Clinics (2026)協助貴診所符合《診所實務守則》（2026年版）

Our maintenance, calibration and validation services are aligned with the Department of Health's Code of Practice for Clinics (2026 edition), helping your facility meet its equipment and infection-control obligations.我們的保養、校準及驗證服務參照衞生署《診所實務守則》（2026年版），協助貴診所履行設備管理及感染控制方面的責任。

Code — Section 2守則 — 第2節

Environment & facility management環境及設施管理

  • Suitable physical design and good ventilation.適合的實體設計及良好通風系統。
  • Clean, hygienic facilities with adequate lighting.設施清潔衞生，並有充足照明。
  • Devices and equipment kept in good working order.裝置和設備保持運作良好。
  • Facilities comply with relevant ordinances and regulations.設施符合相關條例及規例。
Code — Section 2.2守則 — 第2.2節

Equipment & storage設備及貯存

  • Provide the necessary equipment and use it for its intended purpose.準備所需設備，並按原本用途使用。
  • Equipment in good working order and well maintained (e.g. autoclave validation).設備性能良好、保養得宜（如 Autoclave validation）。
  • Keep proper maintenance and servicing records.妥善保存維修及保養紀錄。
  • Single-use devices must not be reused.一次性設備不可重複使用。
Code — Section 4守則 — 第4節

Infection control感染控制措施

  • All staff follow standard infection-control and prevention measures.全體人員遵照標準感染控制及預防措施。
  • Refer to international and local guidelines (e.g. Infection Control Guide).參考國際及本地指引（如 Infection Control Guide）。
  • Suitable, adequate PPE available.備有合適、充足的個人防護設備（PPE）。
  • All sterilising equipment regularly inspected, maintained and documented, with staff trained in its use.須定期檢查及保養所有滅菌設備並妥為記錄，相關職員須就滅菌設備的使用接受合適訓練。

Information referenced from the Department of Health, Code of Practice for Clinics (2026 edition). This summary is provided for general guidance only and does not replace the official Code.資料參考自衞生署《診所實務守則》2026年版。本摘要僅供一般參考，並不取代官方守則。
